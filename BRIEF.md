# Self-authored under explicit creative delegation

## Creative brief

1. Vibe: ultra-modern, editorial, high-fashion minimalism with warm celebratory elegance; ivory, sand, espresso, champagne. Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans. Photography-led, distinct scene cuts.
2. Journey: arrive in a dimensional editorial scene; discover four kinds of celebrations; see how care moves from vision to celebration; browse real-feeling event stories; hear from clients; begin an inquiry.
3. Energy curve: calm opening, curious exploration, focused process, expansive portfolio, intimate client voices, confident invitation.
4. Desired feeling: welcome, possibility, confidence, delight, trust, resolve. Peak: a selected celebration's theme flows into the inquiry the moment a visitor presses “Plan something like this.”
5. Distinctive experience: a portfolio detail can seed the inquiry form with its theme and category, connecting inspiration to a useful next step.
6. Range: premium-minimal editorial, with warm layered photography.
7. Structure: distinct scenes in a gallery/catalog grammar, with an editorial title page and a restrained pinned process chapter.
8. Assets: none supplied. Curated remote Unsplash image URLs and Google Fonts are used as replaceable sample assets.

## Audience and action

This is a concept brand for couples, hosts and teams looking for a considered celebration. By the end, a visitor should believe their event can feel personal while its details are handled with care. The single action is “Begin a conversation.”

## Page grammar and signature

Gallery/catalog: the page is a walkable collection, with filtering and event detail drawers. An editorial hero and a compact pinned process passage are supporting scenes. Other grammars fit less well because the brief centers on browsing varied celebrations and carrying a chosen story into the inquiry. Signature move: “Plan something like this” seeds the inquiry notes with portfolio metadata.

## Journey and feeling curve

1. Welcome: a layered floral dining scene establishes intimate scale.
2. Curiosity: service preview images update as the visitor chooses a celebration.
3. Confidence: four anchored steps progress through a pinned visual scene.
4. Delight: event photographs can be filtered, opened, and carried into an inquiry.
5. Intimacy: client words pair with large, warm photography.
6. Resolve: a short validated inquiry opens a clear confirmation state.

Peak sentence: “I found an event that felt like ours, and its idea was already waiting in the inquiry.”

## Hero layer contract

Far plane: full-bleed garden dinner photograph, slowest upward movement. Midplane: muted champagne light wash and title framing. Focal plane: title and CTA remain fixed in the hero's visual plane. Near plane: floating inset table-detail photograph drifts slightly faster. Atmosphere: subtle grain and warm veil. Essential text is semantic HTML and sits on a restrained dark scrim. On mobile, decorative inset imagery is removed and type stays above the main photo.

## Scroll score

| Beat | Device | Span | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Hero | flow + custom layered parallax | 1.15 | Photographic welcome, no heavy pinned takeover |
| Services | flow + interactive image selection | 1.1 | Visitor selects the kind of celebration |
| Process | pin | 3.0 | Shared visual holds while each stage progresses |
| Portfolio | pan | 4.4 desktop | Breadth becomes a lateral collection; native filtered grid on mobile |
| Stories | flow + staggered reveal | 1.0 | Intimate social proof, gentle cut |
| Inquiry | flow | 1.0 | A useful, low-friction action and resolved ending |

At least four distinct device families are used: flow, parallax, pin, pan and reveal. No adjacent acts repeat a device family. Signature move is bespoke page logic, not a device parameter.
